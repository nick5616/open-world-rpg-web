# CR 041: Base building (playtest 3)

Branch `pt3/base-building`. Playtest feedback: "Make it like the world is breakable like Minecraft, and building your own base is a lot more desirable." Chopping, breaking and digging belong to `pt3/world-edits` (CR 040: logs, stone, dirt, resin); this CR is the **building**: a hammer, a catalogue of pieces, and the things that make a base worth coming home to.

It **extends the camps** (CR 025) instead of replacing them: a building piece is a camp piece (same `camp_pieces` table, same events 1100-1102, same chest, same collision twin, same area streaming, same WP16 zone ownership), and the five camp kits keep working exactly as before.

## Summary

- **Hammer / build mode** (key **B**, a Build button on the camp menu): a ghost snapped to the 2 m grid and its edges, green or red with the reason; **R** or the wheel turns it a quarter, **Q / E** (or the hotkeys 1-9 over the category row) pick the piece, **Tab** the material, **left mouse** places, **right mouse** or Esc leaves. **Hold X** on one of your pieces deconstructs it (80 % of the materials back), **F** repairs it, **E** uses it (a door opens, a bed rests, a chest opens).
- **Pieces** (`contracts/build.yaml`): foundation, floor, wall, half wall, window wall, door, stairs, ramp, roof slope, ridge cap, gable, pillar, beam, fence, gate, each in **wood, stone and sunsteel-reinforced stone** (ids `100 + 4 * piece + material`); furnishings (ids 200+): bench, table, rug, banner, lantern, wall torch, trophy mount (comfort), **bed**, **large chest** (32 slots), **home forge**, **kitchen hearth** (cooking), **carpenter's bench** (the camp workbench's recipes), **warding hearth**.
- **Grid and sockets**: cells are 2 m, a level is 2.5 m; positions are whole metres (half metres for furnishings), rotations quarter turns. Pieces connect through sockets (matching points): a wall stands on a foundation or floor edge, a floor rests on wall, pillar or beam tops (or is held by a neighbouring floor up to 2 cells from a real support), a roof's low edge rests on a wall top, wall decor hangs on a wall face. **Simple structural support**: every piece must connect to the ground (a foundation, or a ground-anchored piece on the ground) through sockets; a deconstruct that would leave other pieces hanging is refused ("holds up N pieces").
- **A base worth coming home to**: the **bed** is your respawn point and rests you (Rested) with a **comfort score** from furnishings within 12 m (each kind counts up to twice): +2.5 % Rested duration and +2 % health regen per point (tiers Bare, Cosy, Homely, Luxurious); **Return home** from the bed (Interact 90/12): a 5-minute cooldown, not after a fight, never while downed. The **warding hearth** never goes out: demons never rise within 20 m, never step into that circle and leave players inside alone (the campfire's night rules), and it **mends** your pieces slowly. Pieces **weather** (wood 1, stone 0.4, sunsteel 0.1 percent per in-game day, never below 20 %) except inside a hearth's ward; **repair** restores a piece for 30 % of its cost scaled by the wear. Lanterns, wall torches, the forge, the kitchen hearth and the hearth glow (emissive cards plus the camp layer's pooled lights: the nearest few; never a three.js light per piece).
- **Ownership**: every piece has an owner; others cannot build onto, use, repair or deconstruct it. **Share with party** (Interact 90/13, the build menu) lets your party's members build next to, use, repair and deconstruct your pieces (and open your doors). Pieces are not attackable by players at all (no raiding in v1; demons are kept off by the ward).
- **Limits**: 450 build pieces per character, 1600 per 256 m area (the spatial bucket the zone already indexes by), at most the per-kind `max` of furnishings; not in or within 24 m of a settlement, 12 m of a site, 5 m of a road; not underground; at most 12 m above the ground; the solid set is rebuilt once per tick however many pieces change.

## Contract changes

- **New `contracts/build.yaml`** (appended into the camps tables by `contracts/codegen/lib/build.mjs`): `CAMPS.pieces` now holds the five camps (ids 1-5) then the build pieces (ids 100-163, 200-212); a piece has `build: {cat, base, mat, par, anchor, gpt, boxes, openBoxes, door, surface, occ, up, down, side, face, reach, coexist, cost, comfort, use, slots, light}` (all geometry in integer centimetres, local frame) and `CAMPS.build` has the numbers (budgets, margins, refund, repair, weather, foundation tolerances, hearth, comfort tiers, home cooldown, materials). **`CAMPS.pieces[id - 1]` no longer works: look a piece up by `id`** (sparse ids).
- **items.yaml** (additive, no version bump): categories `stone` and `earth` (stack 99), items `wood:log`, `wood:resin`, `stone:block`, `earth:dirt` (the breakable world's drops; CR 040 yields them). Costs are items, not kits.
- **Events 1820-1829** (range 1820-1839): 1820 `build_info`, 1821 `build_missing`. **Event 1100/1101 record version 2**: two bytes appended (`state`, `condition`), flags bit 16 = you may modify it. **Event 1103 reasons 24-34** (below). **Event 1105** `data` gains `comfort` and `tier`.
- **Interact 90** (camp) actions: 1 place (building kinds: `arg` = height cm + 1048576, 0 = let the zone choose a foundation's height), 2 pick up (a building piece is **deconstructed** for its refund), 3 use (door/gate toggle, bed, large chest, forge, kitchen hearth, carpenter's bench), **11 repair** (`targetId` = piece id), **12 home**, **13 share** (`arg` 0 / 1), 14 resend 1820 (10 `sync` also sends it). No new protocol message (74 unused).
- **Migration 0032**: `camp_pieces.state smallint`, `.cond real` and `.cond_day real` (condition and the world day it was stamped), so doors keep their state and wear persists.
- Refusal reasons: 24 not on the grid, 25 inside a site, 26 on a road, 27 too high, 28 nothing to rest on or hang from, 29 you have placed as many pieces as you may, 30 this land has all the pieces it can carry, 31 missing materials (Event 1821 lists them), 32 other pieces rest on it, 33 no bed to go home to, 34 too soon (home cooldown).

## Wire

**Piece record, version 2** (Event 1100 header version byte 2; Event 1101): the v1 record (`u64 id | u8 kind | u8 rot | i32 x, y, z cm | u8 flags | u32 ms | u8 chest slots used | u8 len + owner | u8 len + label`) followed by `u8 state` (bit 0 open: doors, gates) and `u8 condition` (percent, 0-100, wear already applied). Flags: 1 yours, 2 a chest shared with its owner's party, 4 its owner is in your party, 8 burning (campfires; the warding hearth always, `ms` 0), **16 you may modify it** (yours, or shared by a party member).

**1820 `build_info`** (to you after join, on every change to your pieces, your share setting, your comfort, your bed): `value` = your build pieces, `data` = JSON `{pieces, budget, share, areaUsed, areaBudget, comfort: {score, max, tier, next: {score, name} | null}, home: {x, y, z} | null, homeCdMs, hearth: {x, z, r} | null}`.
**1821 `build_missing`** (with refusal 31): `targetId` = kind, `data` = JSON `[{item, qty, have}]`.

**Cost formulas** (the client mirrors them for the menu): placing costs `piece.build.cost`; deconstruct returns `floor(qty * refundPct / 100)` of each line; repair costs `max(1, ceil(qty * repairPct / 100 * (100 - condition) / 100))` of each line (nothing when the condition is 100).

**Collision twin**: the solid set now carries the building pieces: per piece the boxes `BoxesOf` (a door's depend on `state`), and walkable **surfaces** (foundations, floors, stairs, ramps) that `Level.HeightAt` returns when `top <= feet + MAX_STEP_UP_M` (`camps.Set.SurfaceAt`; TS `CampSet.surfaceAt`); `camps.With(inner, set, y)` and the TS `CampLevel` take the level view's feet height. Golden vectors: `server/pkg/camps/testdata/build.json` (placements and surface heights/pushes over a stone-and-wood house), replayed by `client/src/ui/build/rules.test.ts` (`go test ./server/pkg/camps -run TestBuildVectors -update` writes them).
