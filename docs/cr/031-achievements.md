# CR 031: Achievements (playtest 2)

Branch `pt2/achievements`. Nicole: "add a whole achievement system, add "Tony Stark built this in a cave with scraps" that one quote from Iron Man, for when you take flight using magma dash".

## Summary

- **One table**: `contracts/achievements.yaml` (`npm run gen` → `CONTENT.achievements` for the client, `contentdefs.D.Achievements` for the zone). **119 achievements** in nine categories (Exploration 18, Combat 21, Magic 19, Crafting & Gathering 19, Social 8, Companions 10, Music 7, World Events & Dungeons 11, Secrets 6), each with a name, description, category, tier (bronze 10 / silver 25 / gold 50 / platinum 100 points, overridable with `pts`), a counter and its goal, gold and XP (defaults by tier), an optional nameplate **title** (56 of them) and/or **item** reward (five: the Tony Stark scraps, potions, a brilliant moonstone, starmetal), a **hidden** flag (shown as "???" with an optional hint until earned) and **chains** (steps that count one stat, shown I / II / III in count order: 30 chains, 74 steps).
- **Migration of the feats**: the 37 feats of CR 023 and the later features (fishing, pets, instruments) moved into the table under the same keys, stats and counts (`internal/content/achievements_test.go` pins all 37), so every earned feat, title and counter carries over. `content.yaml` no longer has `feats:` (the generator refuses it). The character's content state (`character_content.data`, jsonb, CR 023) keeps the earned keys in `feats` and gains `at` (when each was earned) and version 2. A state saved before CR 031 loads as it was; its first check earns, in one go, every new achievement its old counters already reach (kills, tablets, vaults found, fish by species, duels...) and tells the player once (Event 1241, one toast, not one each), then saves as version 2. **No SQL migration**: the counters ride in the existing jsonb and are written through the existing write-behind queue (coalesced per character, at most every 5 s), so 0029 stays the latest.
- **Counters** (server-authoritative; `server/zone/internal/game/achievements.go` hooks, the content state's own maps): kills (and by combat family and by element), demons, bosses (the boss family), spells cast in total and per school (`cast:<element>`, and `schools` cast), dashes started, metres dashed per dash (`dashm:<dash>`), magma jet flight (seconds high up, best height, flights), magma dives that landed, deaths, players downed in open PvP, duels won, lightning strikes survived (and bolts your own metal weapon drew), nights seen through from dusk to dawn, gold earned, items found by rarity, things crafted (and per station: forge, cook, workbench, camp), ore veins broken, herbs, fish (and by species, kinds, legendaries), towers attuned, regions walked, vaults found and cleared, lore tablets, bestiary entries, level, side quests, stories, bounties, towns honored, emotes, creatures tamed (kinds, shiny, the best pet's level), pets petted, metres ridden on your steed, notes, songs, ovations, fireside songs, world events (and by kind, and kinds won). Per-tick quantities (dash distance, airtime, metres ridden) add up in the session and reach the state once a second; everything else is one map increment where it happens. A check (`NewAchievements`) runs once a second at most, only when something changed.
- **"Tony Stark built this in a cave with scraps"** (Secrets, gold, hidden with the hint "Some fire is for burning. Some is for flying.", title **the Ironclad**, 150 gold, 600 XP and five iron ingots: the scraps). Earned the first time you **take flight** on Magma Dash: the zone paid the hold (CR 021's `sustainDash`: the key is held, the jet thrusting, gravity cancelled) and your feet stay more than **6 m above the ground or the water** under you for **over 2 s in a row**, in one dash. A burst (tap) never counts; a hold along the ground never counts. The zone checks at once (not at the next second), so the toast arrives while you are still up there. Its siblings: **Icarus Was an Amateur** (60 m up on a jet, hidden, gold), **Frequent Flyer** (a minute of high jet flight), **Afterburner** (2 km jetted), **Superhero Landing** (a magma dive that leaves a crater, hidden).
- **Unlocks**: Event 1002 (`achievement_earned`, CR 023's `feat_earned` with more fields) to the player only, with the rewards paid as one grant marked `feat:<key>` (the CR 023 marker: nothing is paid twice). Gold and platinum unlocks are also told to the earner's party on the zone and players within 64 m (Event 1240), shown as a short line, not a toast.
- **Client** (see below): the Achievements screen (Y), the unlock toast with a fanfare, titles in the character panel.

## Contract changes

- `contracts/achievements.yaml` (new) and `contracts/codegen/lib/content.mjs`: `parseContent(doc, combat, items, achievements)`. The generator checks every stat and its argument (`family`, `killel`, `cast`, `dashm`, `craft`, `fish`, `loot`, `event`), items, chains (two or more steps, one stat, distinct counts), unique names, a hint only on hidden ones, and that no goal is out of reach (fish kinds, tameable kinds, pet level cap, bestiary size, tablets, schools, event kinds). Output: `achievements`, `achievementCats`, `achievementTiers` (`feats` removed; TS `AchievementDef` replaces `FeatDef`, Go `contentdefs.Achievement` replaces `Feat`). Ids are table order (1-based) and only travel on the wire next to keys; the saved state uses keys.
- **Events 1240-1259** (new range; 1242-1259 reserved): 1240 `achievement_announce`, 1241 `achievements_caught_up`. **1002** renamed `achievement_earned`: `value` = points total, `data` adds `tier, cat, pts, item, qty, at`. **1000** adds `earned` (key → unix ms) and `points`; `progress` covers every achievement except hidden ones not yet earned.
- **Interact 80 / 5** (title): `targetId` = achievement id.
- No protocol version change, no SQL migration (state v2 inside the CR 023 jsonb).

## Wire and budget

Nothing per tick. An unlock is one 1002 (~250 B) to you; a gold or platinum one adds one 1240 (~30 B) to your party and players within 64 m. The sync (1000) grows by the `earned` map and ~120 progress numbers (~3 KB), sent on join and when the journal or the Achievements screen asks. Content is off in atlas-less worlds (the flat test and G5 scale worlds: no `contentSess`), so every hook is a nil check there and `LODESTONE_WIRE_DIGEST=1` is identical (STATUS has the hashes). Zone cost with content on: one nil check per player per tick, plus a height lookup per tick for a player on a magma jet; one map write per counted thing; a flush and maybe a check once a second per player.

## Known gaps

- Counters start at CR 031 for the things nobody counted before (casts, dashes, deaths, nights, gold, crafting, mining, riding...); only what the content state already kept (kills, families, tablets, vaults, fish, pets, quests...) counts from the start.
- Nights count only on the zone that saw both the dusk and the dawn (a handoff in between restarts the night); "survived" means seen through, not "never went down".
- Gold earned counts grants (loot, quests, events, vault chests, achievement rewards), not merchant sales; items found count every grant with a rarity except purchases, crafting and picking up a dropped item.
- The announcement reaches the party on the same zone only (like the kill feed); the screen polls the sync every 5 s while open rather than streaming progress.
- All names are proposals for Nicole (`contracts/achievements.yaml`).
