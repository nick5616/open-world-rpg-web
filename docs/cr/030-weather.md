# CR 030: Dynamic weather (playtest 2)

Branch `pt2/weather`. Nicole, overnight: "all sorts of good shit" (Valheim and Breath of the Wild). Weather that changes by region and biome, storms with lightning that respects what you hold, snow that slows you and cold that bites at night, wind that carries the glider and arrows, fog that hides you, and a sky, ground and soundscape that show all of it.

## Summary

- **Weather states** (`server/pkg/weather/states.go` = `client/src/systems/weather/weather.ts`): clear, cloudy, windy, fog, drizzle, rain, thunderstorm, snow, snowstorm, heat haze, sandstorm. The world is cut into 8 x 8 cells of `WEATHER_CELL_M` (512 m). Each cell has a **climate** voted from the atlas biome grid (4 x 4 samples: 4+ snowpeak samples make it cold, else 5+ badlands arid, else the commonest biome: meadow and forest temperate, wetland wet, highlands highland). The official world: 38 temperate, 8 wet, 10 highland, 3 cold, 5 arid cells. Cells group into **fronts** of 2 x 2 (a region). A front keeps a severity roll (0-99) for `WEATHER_WINDOW_S` (10 min), its windows starting at a seeded phase so fronts change at different moments; each cell nudges the roll by up to `WEATHER_JITTER` (7). The cell's climate table turns the roll into a state, ordered fair to foul, so one storm front is a thunderstorm over the meadows, a snowstorm on the peaks next to them and a sandstorm in the badlands. Heat haze shows only 09:00-18:00. Tables (percent): temperate clear 34, cloudy 18, windy 8, fog 7, drizzle 11, rain 13, thunderstorm 9; wet 18/16/4/18/16/17/11; highland 28/18/17/10/6/11/10; cold clear 26, cloudy 18, windy 10, fog 8, snow 26, snowstorm 12; arid clear 32, heat haze 24, cloudy 8, windy 14, sandstorm 14, rain 4, thunderstorm 4.
- **Deterministic and shared**: weather is a pure function of (seed, chemistry step, cell): u32 hashes, floor divisions and one float hour. Go and TS replay `server/pkg/weather/testdata/weather_vectors.json` (states for 4 seeds x 9 steps x 64 cells, windows, rolls, phases, next states, movement fields, arrow drift, 1,294 lightning strikes and their tall spots). **Nothing is sent per tick.**
- **Smooth transitions** (client only): a front's change crossfades over `WEATHER_BLEND_S` (90 s), centred on the change (gameplay switches at the middle). Near a cell border the neighbour bleeds in over 90 m. Everything is eased again at the camera (~2.5 s).
- **Forecast**: the HUD clock pill shows the weather where you stand (icon + name) and a line under it when it changes within 4 minutes ("Storm rolling in soon", "Clearing in 3 min", "Snow in 2 min", "Blizzard coming soon"). Solo play shows the same pill.
- **Thunderstorms**: each thunderstorm cell rolls for a strike every `LIGHTNING_SLOT_S` (3 s) with `LIGHTNING_SLOT_PCT` (35 %), at a seeded spot and step. **Tall things draw it**: the bolt lands on the highest of nine points on a 12 m grid around its spot (peaks, towers, roofs). **Players draw it**: a player in the open within `LIGHTNING_ATTRACT_M` (10 m), or `LIGHTNING_METAL_ATTRACT_M` (30 m) holding a sword or spear, plus half their height above the ground (a glider high up, a tower top), is struck instead (the closest relative to their reach). A player holding metal in a storm also **draws bolts of their own** (`LIGHTNING_METAL_PCT`, 6 % a slot, about one every 50 s): their weapon crackles 2 s ahead (Event 1221, a HUD warning). Switch to a bow or staff, or get under cover, and it lands 30 m away instead. A bolt deals `LIGHTNING_DAMAGE` (45, armor and shields apply, a respawn grace protects; it can down you), half to players and enemies within 4 m. It **sets dry grass alight** through the chemistry engine (an ignite op like a fire spell's; the rain usually douses it within seconds). Caves and settlements shelter (towns' roofs and towers take the bolts).
- **Server authority**: the zone resolves seeded strikes only where a player can see them (within 260 m). It sends Event 1220 to the players within `LIGHTNING_EVENT_M` (260 m), with the target, damage and flags. Clients draw seeded strikes farther than `LIGHTNING_LOCAL_M` (200 m) from them on their own, from the seed (deduplicated by strike id), so a distant storm costs no bytes. Thunder plays from each strike's distance at the speed of sound (the audio QoL pass's `thunderFor`), with a crack for strikes within 450 m. The flash lights the sky, the clouds, the fog and the hemisphere light for a blink.
- **Gameplay**: rain (drizzle, rain, thunderstorm where you stand) keeps WP10's climbing rule (half speed, slips). **Snow slows walking and sprinting** (`SNOW_MOVE_MULT` 0.9, `SNOWSTORM_MOVE_MULT` 0.8). **Cold**: out in a cold-climate cell at night, or in a snowstorm, for `COLD_ONSET_S` (20 s) without warmth makes you **Chilled**: no health regeneration, mana at `COLD_MANA_REGEN_MULT` (0.6). Warmth is a burning campfire or a town hearth/campfire within `COLD_WARMTH_M` (9 m), a settlement, a cave, Rested, or **warm food** (stew, emberroot skewer, frostcap broth, fisherman's stew, frostgill chowder, ember curry, seafarer's pie, grilled meat: `COLD_FOOD_WARM_S`, 10 min). Event 1222 tells you (HUD: "Freezing", "Chilled", "Warm 9m"). **Wind**: windy weather and thunderstorms (level 2) and snowstorms and sandstorms (level 3) push a glider downwind by `WIND_GLIDE_PUSH_MS` (1.25 m/s) per level above 1, along the chemistry wind's direction, in the shared movement step (TS = Go, goldens). Arrows drift downwind by `WIND_ARROW_ACCEL` (0.6 m/s²) per level. **Fog**: fog banks, snowstorms and sandstorms cut enemies' aggro range to `FOG_AGGRO_MULT` (0.6) and visibility to ~100 m.
- **Visuals** (every style; `world/sky/weatherStyle.ts` sets per-style bolt width and brightness, flash, rainbow, fog-bank density, storm darkness, flake size and square flakes): cloud cover and a storm darkening of the whole dome; **fog banks** (patchy, drifting, ground-hugging density in the fog pass, which also swallows the sky); **snow** (GL points that write depth; Retro's are chunky squares) and **snow settling** on upward-facing terrain over about a minute, melting over three; **puddles and a wet sheen** in level hollows while the ground is wet (it dries over ~2 minutes); **blowing sand** (an ochre stream and ochre air); **heat shimmer** (a UV wobble in the fog pass); **rainbows** (primary and a faint secondary bow at 42° and 51° from the antisolar point) on about half of the clearings after a shower, in the first 4 minutes, with the sun up but below 42°; **lightning bolts** (jagged branching ribbons, seeded by strike id so everyone sees the same shape, HDR so bloom catches them). The grass bends harder in a gale. Low quality keeps it cheap: 900 snow / 500 sand points (High 3,000 / 1,600), no extra passes.
- **Audio** (through the audio QoL pass's weather submix, so roofs, walls and caves muffle it): rain level follows the weather (a drizzle is quieter than a downpour); wind rises with the weather's wind and **gusts** in a gale; a **storm** layer (a low roll under 140 Hz); blowing **sand** (a 2-5 kHz hiss); thunder only from real strikes when the zone runs dynamic weather (the random downpour rumbles remain for atlas-less zones). Insects fall silent in snow and gales.

## Contract changes

- **contracts/constants.yaml** v7, group `weather`: `WEATHER_WINDOW_S`, `WEATHER_CELL_M`, `WEATHER_FRONT_CELLS`, `WEATHER_JITTER`, `WEATHER_BLEND_S`, `SNOW_MOVE_MULT`, `SNOWSTORM_MOVE_MULT`, `WIND_GLIDE_PUSH_MS`, `WIND_ARROW_ACCEL`, `LIGHTNING_SLOT_S`, `LIGHTNING_SLOT_PCT`, `LIGHTNING_TALL_PROBE_M`, `LIGHTNING_ATTRACT_M`, `LIGHTNING_METAL_ATTRACT_M`, `LIGHTNING_METAL_PCT`, `LIGHTNING_WARN_STEPS`, `LIGHTNING_DAMAGE`, `LIGHTNING_SPLASH_M`, `LIGHTNING_EVENT_M`, `LIGHTNING_LOCAL_M`, `COLD_ONSET_S`, `COLD_WARMTH_M`, `COLD_FOOD_WARM_S`, `COLD_MANA_REGEN_MULT`, `FOG_AGGRO_MULT`. The WP10 stub constants stay (atlas-less worlds and the clock vectors use them).
- **Events 1220-1239** (new range; 1223-1239 reserved; 1200-1219 are CR 029's pets): 1220 `lightning_strike`, 1221 `lightning_warn`, 1222 `weather_status`.
- **Event 500 `world_clock`**: one byte appended (`data[18]`): 0 = the zone runs no dynamic weather (WP10's global rain, as before), 1 = seeded weather, 2 + s = forced to state s everywhere (dev). Older clients ignore it.
- **Movement step** (`movement.Env` / `StepEnv`): an optional `Weather` field (`weather.MoveField`: rain, speed factor, glider push at a position), read once at the start of each step. Absent, every existing golden is unchanged (regenerated, byte-identical). New goldens `movement_pt2_weather_{snow_walk,gale_glide,storm_climb}.json` (TS-generated, Go-replayed within 1e-9 and the discrete state exactly). The glide speed bound allows the gale's push.
- **Chemistry**: `chem.Sim.Weather` / `ChemSim.weather`: rain per burning cell from its weather cell (each weather cell evaluated once per step). nil keeps WP10's global windows, so the five existing vectors are unchanged; a sixth (`weather-rain`, three fires in a wet climate, one under a thunderstorm) is replayed by vitest.
- No protocol change, no migration, no Interact. Dev only: `LODESTONE_ZONE_DEBUG_WEATHER=<state>` (`LODESTONE_ZONE_DEBUG_RAIN=1|0` now means rain / clear everywhere), `LODESTONE_ZONE_NO_WEATHER`, `_NO_LIGHTNING`, `_NO_COLD`; client `?weather=<state>`, `__lodestone.setWeather/weather/weatherStrike/setRainbow`.

## Wire and budget

Weather, wind, snow, fog, rainbows and distant lightning are computed from the seed on every client: nothing per tick. A strike that could hit a player is one Event 1220 (~25 bytes) to the players within 260 m; a metal draw adds one 1221 to the player and those within 64 m; cold status is one 1222 to you on change. Worlds without an atlas (the flat test world and the G5 scale worlds) get none of it (`Config.Weather` nil: WP10's global rain stub), so `LODESTONE_WIRE_DIGEST=1` is **identical** to main (see STATUS for the hashes and `BenchmarkScale`). Zone cost with weather on: one field of 64 cells per chemistry step (5 Hz), 64 slot rolls per 3 s, a distance check per player per strike near players, a once-a-second cold check per player.

## Known gaps

- Weather does not reach underground in the step (rain still makes a cave wall slippery in a storm above, as WP10's global rain did; snow slows you in a snowpeak cave). Shelter from lightning and cold is a cave or a settlement, not any roof: a porch in the wild does not shelter.
- Snow settles on terrain only (not on trees, rocks, roofs or players); puddles do not reflect; fog banks follow the camera's altitude rather than the valleys.
- Region elements do not flavour the weather yet (spec 7's "lightning storms over topaz highlands"); `stormFrequency` in the WorldSpec does not scale the tables.
- Zone-resolved strikes reach clients after their latency (the bolt you see near you is ~RTT/2 late); seeded strikes between 200 and 260 m can be drawn twice only if the ids differ (they don't: deduplicated by id).
- Wind drift on arrows is client-side only; the zone's 30° aim slack covers it (≤ 2° at full bow range in a gale).
