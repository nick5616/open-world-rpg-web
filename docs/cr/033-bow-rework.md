# CR 033: Bow rework (playtest 2)

Branch `pt2/bow`. Nicole: "bows are fucked. they dont pull all the way back and fire basically instantly. should be able to hold it mouse1 and then let go mouse1 it fires, infinite ammo".

## Summary

- **Controls.** With a bow, the **left mouse button** draws while held and letting go shoots. A full draw takes **1 s** (`bow_charged.chargeMs`, unchanged) and hits hardest (x2.4 damage), flies fastest (45 to 75 m/s) and farthest. A tap still draws **150 ms** (`minChargeMs`, new) before it looses a weak shot (x1.21 damage, about 54 m reach); there is no instant shot any more. A draw pressed during the last shot's recovery starts once it is over, so a release is never lost. **Z** (or the right button with the weapon picked on the wheel) **lowers the draw** without shooting; the left button must come up before the next draw. The right button with a spell picked still casts; Tab (arrow rain) is unchanged. The sword, spear, staff and wand are unchanged (the spear still charges on Z).
- **On the wire nothing new.** The draw is the existing `alt_attack` input bit, counted per frame exactly like the spear charge and the old Z draw; the release is the `attack` bit on the first frame without it. The zone (`combat/player.go startMove`) already derives `bow_charged` and its charge (`altHeld / chargeSteps`) from those frames; it now also **refuses** a bow attack with no draw (the old `bow_quick` light move, kept in the table only so its id still classifies) and a release drawn for fewer than `minChargeSteps` (9; one step of tolerance). A full-power shot therefore needs the full second of draw frames on the wire.
- **Reach follows the draw.** `ShotRange(m, c) = reach x ShotSpeed(m, c) / chargeSpeed` (Go `combat/spellsim.go`, TS `timeline.ts shotRange`): a full draw reaches 90 m, a tap about 54 m (the same flight time at the lower speed). Hit validation (`validate.go geometryOK`) checks a claim against it; your arrow (`runtime.launch`) and other players' arrows (`fx/engine.ts`) fly that far.
- **Infinite ammo.** Shots never spent arrows (WP13 v1) and still do not; now it is the rule. Using a stack of arrows only fits those arrowheads (`Loadout.ArrowOre`: damage multiplier, sunsteel kills demons) to every shot from then on, and the stack is kept. The tooltip says so ("Use: fit Iron arrowheads to your bow (bows never run out of arrows; the stack is kept)"), the inventory button is "Fit arrowheads", and the Controls say bows never run out of arrows. There was no "no arrows" refusal to remove.
- **First person.** The bow rises to an aiming hold as the draw starts (canted, below the crosshair, `BOW_AIM`) and the string and nocked arrow come back step by step with the draw (`timeline.drawFrac`) all the way to `BOW_MAX_DRAW` (0.24 m before, 0.45 m now) with the string hand at the cheek anchor (`BOW_ANCHOR`, its top at the bottom edge of the view) and the elbow out to the right; a held full draw trembles a little (more the longer it is held); the release snaps the string forward and rings it down (`stringAfterRelease`), kicks the bow and lets the hand follow through; the bow lowers 0.4 s later. A small ring round the crosshair (`hud/DrawMeter.tsx`) fills with the draw and turns gold at full draw.
- **Others see the real draw.** The guard start (Event 214, `CastFXGuard`) already went out when the draw began; the bow's held draw is now **aimed**: Event 226 carries the archer's aim while they draw (10 Hz at most, on change, like a stream), so observers point the drawn arrow where the archer looks; a parry or spear charge still sends only keepalives. A late observer starts the pose as far into the draw as Event 226's progress says. The avatar's draw grows linearly over the same 1 s (`BOW_DRAW_MS`), never times out (was 3 s), trembles at full draw, and the release looses from the draw it reached. Each archer has their **own copy of the bow geometry**, so the string comes back with the drawing hand and an arrow sits on it while drawing (before, every third-person bow shared the first-person buffer); the bow stays upright in the raised bow hand (it used to lie along the arm).

## Contract changes

- `contracts/combat.yaml`: `weapons.bow.moves.bow_charged.minChargeMs: 150` (generated `minChargeSteps: 9`; `Move.MinChargeSteps` in `server/pkg/combatdefs`, `minChargeMs/minChargeSteps` on the TS `MoveDef`). Comments: the bow's input semantics and infinite ammo. `contracts/items.yaml`: the arrows comment only.
- No protocol, event, constants or migration change. Event 226 for a bow draw now carries aim updates (its documented "aimed action" list grows by the bow draw; same format).

## Network

Nothing for players who do not use a bow. An archer drawing sends the same input bits as before; observers within 64 m get Event 226 aim updates while they draw (at most 10 Hz, only when the aim moves by ~0.35 degrees, ~25 bytes each), the same budget as a held stream. `LODESTONE_WIRE_DIGEST=1`: the G5 scale scenario's bots only walk (no attacks), so the digest is untouched; the combat bots (`combatbot`) already drew the bow for a full second through `alt_attack` and still do.

## Tests

- Go `server/zone/internal/game/bow_test.go` (real session over WebSocket): a bow shoots with **no arrows** in the bag; using 20 iron arrows fits iron arrowheads and keeps all 20; three drawn shots land and the stack is still 20.
- Vitest `combat/timeline.test.ts` (left button draws with the alt bit and looses at release with charge 1, half draw 0.5, `drawFrac` capped at 1, a tap draws 9 steps then looses at 0.15 and never starts the light move, a draw held through recovery starts after it, Z lowers the draw and the next press draws again, shot speed/reach = the zone's, the spear unchanged), `combat/weapons/hands.test.ts` (a full draw puts the string hand at the cheek anchor ~0.4 m back from rest, in front of the near plane at the bottom edge, the arrow within 10 degrees of the view with its head past the riser, a workable wrist, the string's release ring-down), `net/avatarAnim.test.ts` (the observer's draw follows the hold time, a half-draw release snaps from half, a late observer starts 80% drawn, a 20 s hold stays drawn and trembles), `net/avatar.test.ts` (two archers' strings are independent, drawn all the way, arrow on while drawing, gone after the release), `combat/castState.test.ts` (your hold and their casting bit, left button), `input/bindings.test.ts` (Z lowers the draw, the Controls describe the bow).
