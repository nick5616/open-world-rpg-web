## Summary

- **Light from the heavens.** Every drop of uncommon or better stands in a beam of its rarity's colour: a white-hot thread inside a coloured column inside a soft glow, noise streaks and bright bands pouring *down* into the item, fading into the sky, brightest where it lands, its top swaying with the wind, a gentle pulse (never a flash). A splash ring ripples outward on the ground, motes fall down the beam and spiral onto the item, and every drop (common too) twinkles with a few sparkles. Height and brightness by rarity: uncommon 2.6 m, rare 10, relic 16, legendary 26, mythic 34, primordial 40, Bitchin' 48 m and a cycling rainbow. A new drop's beam strikes down from the sky as it lands. Premultiplied blending keeps the colour against a bright sky; fog dims it but never erases it. Low quality: no motes, one noise octave, one ripple.
- **The real thing on the ground.** Weapons are their real weapon model (type and material from the item key), armor pieces their real armor model (style, material, rarity trim; one piece built at its rest-pose joints), everything else a small model of its own (36 shapes: potion flasks in the potion's colour, bread, a bowl of stew, pies, skewers, a fish platter, meat on the bone, herb bundles, frostcap mushrooms, glowing moss, pelts, seed pouches, fish, ingots in their metal, arrow sheaves, a pickaxe, a rod, a torch, a rope coil, a whetstone, a whistle, kits, a lute, a flute, a drum, handbells, a tome bound in its spell's element colour, a rolled blueprint, a coin pile that grows with the amount, a quest parcel, a key, firewood logs). Common and uncommon things lie on the grass at a slight angle; rare and better float in their beam, slowly turning (swords and spears point down), lit by it. Ores and gems keep WP09's cave models and get the light and hum.
- **More loot on the ground.** Kill loot already dropped as personal pickups (WP14); it now scatters 0.9-2.1 m around the corpse. **Containers** now put only the gold in your purse; every other item pops out of the chest onto the ground around it (on the side facing you). Each new drop arcs out of the corpse or chest on your screen and lands.
- **Walk-over vs E.** Walking over your loot takes gold always and WP09 vein drops always; with **Auto-collect common loot** on (Esc menu, default on) it also takes common and uncommon materials, ore, gems, ingots, arrows, food and potions. Gear, tomes, blueprints, tools and anything rare or better wait for **E** (the prompt names it in its rarity colour). A fresh drop settles 0.8 s before it can be vacuumed, so its arc plays. Taking one near you flies it into your hands.
- **The hum.** Every drop of uncommon or better hums from where it lies, through the audio engine's positional loop pool: weapons, armor, ingots and arrows ring metallic (inharmonic bar partials and a soft ting), gems chime, tomes, blueprints and quest items whisper, everything else is a warm drone, Bitchin' a shimmering chord. Each rarity has its own note (a rising pentatonic) and is louder and carries further: an uncommon ~9 m, a legendary ~34 m, a Bitchin' ~56 m; commons are silent (gems chime softly anyway). Muffled indoors and in caves like the fires; only the loudest 8 loops (fires, water and hums together) get a voice, so the nearest and rarest win. The existing grant sting plays on taking it.

## Ownership (the rule, unchanged from WP14)

- **Rolled loot is personal.** Every eligible player rolls their own kill loot (spec 8) and their own chest contents; those pickups are sent only to their owner (snapshot filter) and only the owner can take them. Nobody can steal your drop, so no reservation timer is needed, and nobody else's rolls clutter your screen (or your downlink).
- **Dropped items are shared.** An item a player drops (G / drag out of the bag) is a public zone entity: every client near it sees the same item, rarity, model and beam in the same place (checked entity by entity with two browsers), and the first E wins. The tick is single-threaded, so two players pressing E cannot both get it; the loser hears "That item is gone" (Event 403 reason 2, remembered for 5 s).
- Despawn as before: 10 minutes (rolled loot and dropped items alike); dropped items persist across a zone restart (WP14), rolled loot does not (a crash can lose an untaken roll, never duplicate one: the chest's open marker commits with its gold, each item is granted once under its own key `container:<id>:<i>` / `kill:...` when taken).

## Wire and server

- **Interact 21/10** `auto-collect setting`, arg 1 on / 0 off. The zone holds it per session (`charItems.autoLoot`, off until the client says) and echoes it in **Event 400 `"auto"`**; the client re-sends its own whenever the echo differs (at most every 4 s), which covers handoffs and rejoins without a persisted field.
- **Kill loot** (`loot.go lootOnKill`): golden-angle scatter (`loot_ground.go scatterOffset`) 0.9-2.1 m, each drop with `readyAt` = now + 0.8 s.
- **Containers** (`loot.go openContainer`): gold into the purse in the container grant (one transaction with the open marker, as before); the rest spawned as personal pickups 1.1-1.9 m from the container over 200 degrees facing the opener. The WP14 "kill mid-grant" process test is unchanged in meaning: the database holds the starter kit plus each container's gold exactly once.
- **Walk-over** (`mining.go miningTick` -> `loot_ground.go autoTake / autoCollects`): the rules above; a full bag leaves the item where it lies and says so at most every 10 s (no toast stream); E always tries and always says why not.
- **Race** (`loot.go takeWorldPickup`): a take of a pickup that a different player took within 5 s answers 403/2.
- **Snapshots** (`snapshots.go`): pickups of rarity >= 2 (`pickupBeamRarity`) ride the **mid ring** (to 200 m, 10 Hz) instead of the near ring only, so their beams are seen from afar; common and uncommon stay near-ring. Pickups are static, so a known one costs almost nothing per frame. Personal pickups still go to their owner only. The scale scenario's bots never fight or loot (no pickups), so `LODESTONE_WIRE_DIGEST=1` is unchanged.

## Client

- `client/src/ui/inventory/ground/beam.ts`: `beamParams(rarity, tier)` and `LootLights`, which draws every drop's beam (instanced billboard ribbons), splash (instanced quads), motes and sparkles (batched points) in **four draw calls** in all, rebuilding its buffers only when the set of lit drops changes. Fog-aware shaders; the four programs (High and Low) are in the shader warm-up (`world/warmExtras.ts`).
- `ground/models.ts`: `dropModelOf(key, rarity)` (kind, shape, pose, holo) and `dropGeometry(key, rarity, qty)`, cached; Bitchin' copies draw holographic with the armor material, hovering copies glow a little. `combat/armor/model.ts armorPieceGeometry` builds one armor piece on its own.
- `ground/hum.ts`: `humFor`, `humKind`, `humEmitters`; `audio/synth.ts` renders the five hum loops (4 s, phase-clean: every frequency a multiple of 1/4 Hz) on first use; `LoopEmitter.rate` sets a voice's pitch; the engine fills in an emitter's acoustic space when a source leaves it NaN.
- `ground/autoLoot.ts` + Esc menu row "Auto-collect common loot"; `link.ts` keeps the zone in step.
- `LootLayer.tsx`: models, pop-out arcs (a pickup with an id newer than anything seen before, within 45 m, arcs out of the batch's centre or the container it surrounds), beam strikes, fly-to-you on take, hum source.
